Rajesh Kumar (K&S Digiprotect): Good morning. Thank you for taking the time for this interview. We are conducting a privacy assessment of the Credit Department as part of ISFC's DPDPA 2023 compliance initiative. Could you start by giving us an overview of your department?

Department SPOC: The credit department of India Shelter Finance Corporation Limited acts as the financial gatekeeper. They assess loan applicants, determine creditworthiness, and approve loans with minimal risk. They also monitor repayments.

Department SPOC: This report encapsulates critical insights for As-Is study, derived from the initial interview conducted with the credit Department of India Shelter Finance Corporation Limited (ISFC) for the implementation of ISO/IEC 27001:2022 and compliance with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDPA). The overarching objectives of this interview were dual fold: first, to gain an understanding of the departmental structure, and second, to delve into the core operational processes of the Credit Department. Additionally, the interview aimed to assess the existing information security practices, focusing on evaluating adherence to DPDPA requirements and the readiness for ISO/IEC 27001:2022 implementation.

Rajesh Kumar (K&S Digiprotect): Does the Credit Department share data with other departments or external parties?

Department SPOC: Yes, we regularly share relevant data with: Compliance Department, Sales Department, Operations Department. This is necessary for cross-departmental coordination and regulatory compliance.

Rajesh Kumar (K&S Digiprotect): What are the primary IT systems and tools used by the Credit Department?

Department SPOC: We primarily use: CRC Vendor, Email, OneDrive, Salesforce. These systems are managed by our IT department and access is controlled through role-based access controls.

Rajesh Kumar (K&S Digiprotect): How does the Credit Department handle consent management for personal data processing?

Department SPOC: Consent is obtained as part of our standard onboarding and data collection processes. However, I acknowledge that we need to formalize our consent management mechanism in line with DPDPA 2023 requirements. This is something we're actively working on with the Compliance team.
