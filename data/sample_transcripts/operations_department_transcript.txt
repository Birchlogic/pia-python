Rajesh Kumar (K&S Digiprotect): Good morning. Thank you for taking the time for this interview. We are conducting a privacy assessment of the Operations Department as part of ISFC's DPDPA 2023 compliance initiative. Could you start by giving us an overview of your department?

Department SPOC: The Operations Department plays an important role in the overall functioning of India Shelter Finance Corporation Limited (ISFC), acting as the central hub for all major operational activities. It is responsible for ensuring seamless coordination among various departments, managing day-to-day operations, and maintaining the efficiency and effectiveness of processes critical to the organization's success. By overseeing functions such as document verification, loan processing, disbursement, loan closures, and field verifications, the Operations Department guarantees that the operational activities comply with regulatory requirements and support the company’s strategic objectives.

Department SPOC: This report encapsulates critical insights for As-Is study, derived from the initial interview conducted with the Operations Department of India Shelter Finance Corporation Limited (ISFC) for the implementation of ISO/IEC 27001:2022 and compliance with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDPA). The overarching objectives of this interview were dual fold: first, to gain an understanding of the departmental structure, and second, to delve into the core operational processes of the Operations Department. Additionally, the interview aimed to assess the existing information security practices, focusing on evaluating adherence to DPDPA requirements and the readiness for ISO/IEC 27001:2022 implementation.

Rajesh Kumar (K&S Digiprotect): Now let's talk about the types of personal data your department collects. What categories of data do you handle, and where is it stored?

Department SPOC: We collect **Loan Application Documents**. Documents submitted by customers for loan application

Rajesh Kumar (K&S Digiprotect): What is the purpose of collecting Loan Application Documents?

Department SPOC: The purpose is: To assess and process home loan applications

Rajesh Kumar (K&S Digiprotect): Where is Loan Application Documents stored?

Department SPOC: Loan Application Documents is stored in iSales App, iCredit App, Salesforce. Its classification is PII/ 
Sensitive. The retention period is As per regulatory requirements. Legal basis: Legitimate Interest, Consent.

Department SPOC: We collect **Physical Verification Data**. Data collected during field visits for document verification

Rajesh Kumar (K&S Digiprotect): What is the purpose of collecting Physical Verification Data?

Department SPOC: The purpose is: To verify the authenticity of submitted documents

Rajesh Kumar (K&S Digiprotect): Where is Physical Verification Data stored?

Department SPOC: Physical Verification Data is stored in Secure physical storage, iCredit App. Its classification is PII/ 
Sensitive. The retention period is As per regulatory requirements. Legal basis: Legitimate Interest, Consent.

Department SPOC: We collect **Loan Agreement Documents**. 40-45 documents involved in the loan agreement process

Rajesh Kumar (K&S Digiprotect): What is the purpose of collecting Loan Agreement Documents?

Department SPOC: The purpose is: To finalize and legally bind the loan agreement

Rajesh Kumar (K&S Digiprotect): Where is Loan Agreement Documents stored?

Department SPOC: Loan Agreement Documents is stored in Scanned and stored in secure digital storage. Its classification is PII/
