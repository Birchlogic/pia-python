Priya Sharma (K&S Digiprotect): Good morning. Thank you for taking the time for this interview. We are conducting a privacy assessment of the Treasury as part of ISFC's DPDPA 2023 compliance initiative. Could you start by giving us an overview of your department?

Treasury is responsible for fund raising from Banks and Financial Institution.

Treasury – Vikas Sharma: They also ensure adequate liquidity and handle monthly ALCO meetings. Loan Accounting Oversees Loan collection, Receipt creation in system, posting accounting entries, insurance premium payments to vendors, refunds/Claims received from Insurance vendors, refunds to borrowers, loan account rescheduling (Part-payment/ROI change/ etc), and reconciliation of funds credited in bank with credit provided in Books. Similarly, oversees, Disbursement approvals, disbursement Payments, posting of accounting entries and its reconciliation.
