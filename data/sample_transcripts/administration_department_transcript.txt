Rajesh Kumar (K&S Digiprotect): Good morning. Thank you for taking the time for this interview. We are conducting a privacy assessment of the Administration Department as part of ISFC's DPDPA 2023 compliance initiative. Could you start by giving us an overview of your department?

Department SPOC: India Shelter’s Admin Department manages office maintenance and support along with overseeing travel arrangements and event logistics, our team is dedicated to upholding efficiency and driving our objectives forward. Additionally, they handle office supplies, rent agreements, courier logistics, and maintain attendance systems. This report encapsulates critical insights for As-Is study, derived from the initial interview conducted with the Administration Department of India Shelter Finance Corporation Limited (ISFC) for the implementation of ISO/IEC 27001:2022 and compliance with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDPA).

Department SPOC: The overarching objectives of this interview were dual fold: first, to gain an understanding of the departmental structure, and second, to delve into the core operational processes of the Administration Department. Additionally, the interview aimed to assess the existing information security practices, focusing on evaluating adherence to DPDPA requirements and the readiness for ISO/IEC 27001:2022 implementation. By delving into the details of the department's organizational setup, operational workflows, and data handling procedures, this report aims to identify both the strengths and areas of improvement in information security management and DPDPA compliance within the department..

Rajesh Kumar (K&S Digiprotect): Now let's talk about the types of personal data your department collects. What categories of data do you handle, and where is it stored?

Department SPOC: We collect **Travel Bookings**. Details of travel bookings for employees

Department SPOC: The purpose is: Facilitate employee travel

Rajesh Kumar (K&S Digiprotect): Where is Travel Bookings stored?

Rajesh Kumar (K&S Digiprotect): Does the Administration Department share data with other departments or external parties?

Department SPOC: Yes, we regularly share relevant data with: Legal Department, HR Department, IT Department, Marketing Department. This is necessary for cross-departmental coordination and regulatory compliance.

Rajesh Kumar (K&S Digiprotect): What are the primary IT systems and tools used by the Administration Department?

Department SPOC: We primarily use: CCTV Server, ESSL, Emails, HR Database, Maintenance Logs, One Drive, Physical Registers, Vendor Database. These systems are managed by our IT department and access is controlled through role-based access controls.

Rajesh Kumar (K&S Digiprotect): How does the Administration Department handle consent management for personal data processing?

Department SPOC: Consent is obtained as part of our standard onboarding and data collection processes. However, I acknowledge that we need to formalize our consent management mechanism in line with DPDPA 2023 requirements. This is something we're actively working on with the Compliance team.
