TRANSCRIPT — Privacy Impact Assessment Interview
Organization: India Shelter Finance Corporation Limited
Department: Human Resources (HR Ops, TA, Training & Development)
Date: HR Ops – 13th March 2024
TA (Area Manager & Above Pan India + Complete HO) – 12th April 2024
TA (Branch Manager & Below), Training & Development – 26th April 2024
Interviewer: Rajesh Kumar (K&S Digiprotect)

======================================================================

Rajesh Kumar (K&S Digiprotect): Good morning. Thank you for taking the time for this interview. We are conducting a privacy assessment of the Human Resources (HR Ops, TA, Training & Development) as part of ISFC's DPDPA 2023 compliance initiative. Could you start by giving us an overview of your department?

HR Ops – Madhu Sharma: The HR Department at India Shelter Finance Corporation Limited (ISFC) facilitates the organization's human capital strategy. Talent Acquisition handles the recruitment process from sourcing candidates to conducting interviews and facilitating the onboarding process, HR ensures the organization attracts and retains top talent vital for driving ISFC's growth and success. ISFC's HR Ops Department oversees a spectrum of functions essential for employee well-being and organizational efficiency.

HR Ops – Madhu Sharma: This includes managing payroll, administering benefits, and maintaining compliance with labour laws and regulatory requirements. Training & Development for employees and external partners is also taken care of by the HR Department. By nurturing a conducive work environment and supporting the diverse needs of its workforce, the HR Department at ISFC contributes significantly to the organization's overall success and sustainability.

Rajesh Kumar (K&S Digiprotect): Let's walk through the key processes in your department. Can you describe each major activity and how data flows through it?

HR Ops – Madhu Sharma: Our first key process is **Recruitment**. Recruitment Process is split into: Area Managers and above pan India along with all HQ hiring is done by Aditya Business team communicates requirement through mails or on calls Candidate Sourcing is done from Job Portals (LinkedIn, Naukri, etc.), through external consultants, and internal references

Rajesh Kumar (K&S Digiprotect): What are the key observations around Recruitment?

HR Ops – Madhu Sharma: Regarding Recruitment, All Resumes are retained indefinitely.
All data is stored on Local machines and/or one drive.
ATS might be enrolled in FY 24-25.
ISFC to confirm the Order of interviews recorded in Talent Acquisition SOP vs On-Ground.
ISFC to explain Re-hiring Proces

HR Ops – Madhu Sharma: I should also note: Documents (KYC Documents, other information required for recruitment) are sometimes received via WhatsApp.
No classification of emails, data, or information is done.
No data deletion policy available/

HR Ops – Madhu Sharma: Another key process is **Background Verification (BGV)**. Background Verification (BGV) is done via external vendors, post joining. This process is taken care of by HR Ops team. All documents are sent on emails to the BGV vendors. Reports are also received via emails.

HR Ops – Madhu Sharma: I should also note: No specific clause for data retention/deletion with Background Verification (BGV) vendors.

HR Ops – Madhu Sharma: Another key process is **Onboarding**. HR Ops team releases Offer Letter to selected candidate. Joining days are Monday & Thursday only. Joining on any other day needs special approval from CHRO. All employees are on-roll. Details for ID Card are shared with Admin Team by HR Ops via Email.

HR Ops – Madhu Sharma: Another key process is **Insurance**. Employee Insurance is taken care of by HR Ops team. There are 4 types of Insurance Policies available for Employees: Individual Mediclaim, Family insurance (parental (add-on)), Accidental, Term.

Rajesh Kumar (K&S Digiprotect): What are the key observations around Insurance?

HR Ops – Madhu Sharma: Regarding Insurance, ISFC to explain Policy renewal process

HR Ops – Madhu Sharma: Another key process is **Budgeting**. Budgeting for Headcount, Payroll Cost, and Non-payroll Cost is done by HR Ops team.

HR Ops – Madhu Sharma: Another key process is **Training**. Training and development are done by VP HR. External Training given to people working in call centres. Training material is given to them, and a confirmation is taken over email about completion of training. Induction program – Parichay is taken by all New Joiners.

Rajesh Kumar (K&S Digiprotect): What are the key observations around Training?

HR Ops – Madhu Sharma: Regarding Training, ISFC to confirm other training programs held for employees.
ISFC to provide details on training calendar.
ISFC to provide training records.
ISFC to confirm training report process.

HR Ops – Madhu Sharma: Another key process is **Employee Exit**. Retention of employees is taken care of by Vijayananda Shenoi

Rajesh Kumar (K&S Digiprotect): What are the key observations around Employee Exit?

HR Ops – Madhu Sharma: Regarding Employee Exit, ISFC to explain entire Exit process.
