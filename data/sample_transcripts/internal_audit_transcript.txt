Rajesh Kumar (K&S Digiprotect): Good morning. Thank you for taking the time for this interview. We are conducting a privacy assessment of the Internal Audit as part of ISFC's DPDPA 2023 compliance initiative. Could you start by giving us an overview of your department?

Internal Audit – Shivam Jain: India Shelter Finance Corporation Limited (ISFC) relies on its Internal Audit Department to ensure that the organization operates in compliance with regulatory standards and internal policies. The department is divided into two key functions: Internal Audit, Field Audit & Anti-Fraud. This team of experienced professionals conducts thorough audits across various departments and branches, employing a structured approach to audit planning and execution.

Internal Audit – Shivam Jain: This report encapsulates critical insights for As-Is study, derived from the initial interview conducted with the Internal Audit Department of India Shelter Finance Corporation Limited (ISFC) for the implementation of ISO/IEC 27001:2022 and compliance with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDPA). The overarching objectives of this interview were dual fold: first, to gain an understanding of the departmental structure, and second, to delve into the core operational processes of the Internal Audit Department. Additionally, the interview aimed to assess the existing information security practices, focusing on evaluating adherence to DPDPA requirements and the readiness for ISO/IEC 27001:2022 implementation.

Rajesh Kumar (K&S Digiprotect): Now let's talk about the types of personal data your department collects. What categories of data do you handle, and where is it stored?

Internal Audit – Shivam Jain: We collect **CDR (Call Detail Record)**. Records for call centre monitoring and performance assessment.

Rajesh Kumar (K&S Digiprotect): What is the purpose of collecting CDR (Call Detail Record)?

Internal Audit – Shivam Jain: The purpose is: Audit

Rajesh Kumar (K&S Digiprotect): Where is CDR (Call Detail Record) stored?

Internal Audit – Shivam Jain: CDR (Call Detail Record) is stored in Salesforce. Its classification is PII/Sensitive Data. The retention period is 8 years. Legal basis: Consent/ Internal Policy.

Internal Audit – Shivam Jain: We collect **KYC Documents**. Customer identity verification documents

Rajesh Kumar (K&S Digiprotect): What is the purpose of collecting KYC Documents?

Internal Audit – Shivam Jain: The purpose is: Audit

Rajesh Kumar (K&S Digiprotect): Where is KYC Documents stored?

Internal Audit – Shivam Jain: KYC Documents is stored in Salesforce. Its classification is PII/Sensitive Data. The retention period is 8 years. Legal basis: Consent/ Internal Policy.

Internal Audit – Shivam Jain: We collect **Loan Documents**. Paperwork for loan disbursements and usage verification audits.

Rajesh Kumar (K&S Digiprotect): What is the purpose of collecting Loan Documents?

Internal Audit – Shivam Jain: The purpose is: Audit

Internal Audit – Shivam Jain: We primarily use: OneDrive Email, Salesforce, Salesforce OneDrive Email. These systems are managed by our IT department and access is controlled through role-based access controls.

Rajesh Kumar (K&S Digiprotect): How does the Internal Audit handle consent management for personal data processing?

Internal Audit – Shivam Jain: Consent is obtained as part of our standard onboarding and data collection processes. Rajesh Kumar (K&S Digiprotect): Based on our assessment so far, we have some preliminary recommendations. Let me share them with you.

Rajesh Kumar (K&S Digiprotect): Recommendation: Ensure that all evidence maintained on Excel is encrypted to protect confidential information from unauthorized access.

Rajesh Kumar (K&S Digiprotect): Recommendation: Include data privacy-related audits in the annual audit charter for both internal and field audits to ensure compliance with data protection regulations.

Rajesh Kumar (K&S Digiprotect): Recommendation: Establish a clear policy to avoid retaining legacy data indefinitely. Retain audit data only for the necessary period as per regulatory requirements.
