Rajesh Kumar (K&S Digiprotect): Good morning. Thank you for taking the time for this interview. We are conducting a privacy assessment of the Sales Department as part of ISFC's DPDPA 2023 compliance initiative. Could you start by giving us an overview of your department?

Department SPOC: The Sales Department of India Shelter Finance Corporation Limited (ISFC) is instrumental in driving the company's growth by acquiring new customers and managing loan disbursements. The team operates across seven zones and handles a significant volume of loan applications, ensuring thorough documentation. This report encapsulates critical insights for As-Is study, derived from the initial interview conducted with the Sales Department of India Shelter Finance Corporation Limited (ISFC) for the implementation of ISO/IEC 27001:2022 and compliance with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDPA).

Department SPOC: The overarching objectives of this interview were dual fold: first, to gain an understanding of the departmental structure, and second, to delve into the core operational processes of the Sales Department. Additionally, the interview aimed to assess the existing information security practices, focusing on evaluating adherence to DPDPA requirements and the readiness for ISO/IEC 27001:2022 implementation. By delving into the details of the department's organizational setup, operational workflows, and data handling procedures, this report aims to identify both the strengths and areas of improvement in information security management and DPDPA compliance within the department..

Rajesh Kumar (K&S Digiprotect): Now let's talk about the types of personal data your department collects. What categories of data do you handle, and where is it stored?

Department SPOC: We collect **Loan Sourcing Data**. Potential Customer References

Rajesh Kumar (K&S Digiprotect): What is the purpose of collecting Loan Sourcing Data?

Department SPOC: The purpose is: Used for lead generation

Rajesh Kumar (K&S Digiprotect): Where is Loan Sourcing Data stored?

Department SPOC: Loan Sourcing Data is stored in iSales/iTrust/Salesforce. Its classification is PII/Sensitive data. Retention period is currently not formally defined. Legal basis: Consent.

Department SPOC: We collect **Customer Info**. Personal information of customers recorded after lead generation
(KYC documents, Bank account details, Property papers and Income documents)

Rajesh Kumar (K&S Digiprotect): What is the purpose of collecting Customer Info?

Department SPOC: The purpose is: Used for Customer identification and loan processing

Rajesh Kumar (K&S Digiprotect): Where is Customer Info stored?

Department SPOC: Customer Info is stored in iSales/iTrust/Salesforce. Its classification is PII/Sensitive data. Retention period is currently not formally defined. Legal basis: Consent.

Department SPOC: We collect **Document Verification Records**. Verified and self-attested documents

Rajesh Kumar (K&S Digiprotect): What is the purpose of collecting Document Verification Records?

Department SPOC: The purpose is: Ensure accuracy and completeness of loan application

Rajesh Kumar (K&S Digiprotect): Where is Document Verification Records stored?

Department SPOC: Document Verification Records is stored in iSales/iTrust/Salesforce/ Physical documents. Its classification is PII/Sensitive data. Retention period is currently not formally defined. Legal basis: Legal Requirement.

Department SPOC: We collect **Customer Interaction Records**. Details of interaction with potential customers

Rajesh Kumar (K&S Digiprotect): What is the purpose of collecting Customer Interaction Records?

Department SPOC: The purpose is: Improve sales pitch/ cold calling efforts

Rajesh Kumar (K&S Digiprotect): Where is Customer Interaction Records stored?

Department SPOC: Customer Interaction Records is stored in Salesforce/ One Drive. Its classification is PII/Sensitive data. Retention period is currently not formally defined. Legal basis: Consent.

Rajesh Kumar (K&S Digiprotect): Does the Sales Department share data with other departments or external parties?

Department SPOC: Yes, we regularly share relevant data with: Legal Department, HR Department, Credit Department, IT Department, Operations Department. This is necessary for cross-departmental coordination and regulatory compliance.

Rajesh Kumar (K&S Digiprotect): What are the primary IT systems and tools used by the Sales Department?

Department SPOC: We primarily use: One Drive, Physical documents, Salesforce, iSales, iTrust. These systems are managed by our IT department and access is controlled through role-based access controls.

Rajesh Kumar (K&S Digiprotect): How does the Sales Department handle consent management for personal data processing?

Department SPOC: Consent is obtained as part of our standard onboarding and data collection processes. However, I acknowledge that we need to formalize our consent management mechanism in line with DPDPA 2023 requirements. This is something we're actively working on with the Compliance team.
