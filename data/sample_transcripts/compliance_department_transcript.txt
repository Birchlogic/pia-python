Rajesh Kumar (K&S Digiprotect): Good morning. Thank you for taking the time for this interview. We are conducting a privacy assessment of the Compliance Department as part of ISFC's DPDPA 2023 compliance initiative. Could you start by giving us an overview of your department?

Department SPOC: The Compliance Department at India Shelter Finance Corporation Limited (ISFC) is dedicated to ensuring that all departments adhere to internal policies, regulatory requirements, and industry standards. It manages compliance checklists, handles non-compliance issues, and coordinates with various departments to maintain accurate records and ensure adherence to compliance policies. This report encapsulates critical insights for As-Is study, derived from the initial interview conducted with the Compliance Department of India Shelter Finance Corporation Limited (ISFC) for the implementation of ISO/IEC 27001:2022 and compliance with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDPA).

Department SPOC: The overarching objectives of this interview were dual fold: first, to gain an understanding of the departmental structure, and second, to delve into the core operational processes of the Compliance Department. Additionally, the interview aimed to assess the existing information security practices, focusing on evaluating adherence to DPDPA requirements and the readiness for ISO/IEC 27001:2022 implementation. By delving into the details of the department's organizational setup, operational workflows, and data handling procedures, this report aims to identify both the strengths and areas of improvement in information security management and DPDPA compliance within the department..
