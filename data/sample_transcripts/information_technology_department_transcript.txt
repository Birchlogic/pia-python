Rajesh Kumar (K&S Digiprotect): Good morning. Thank you for taking the time for this interview. We are conducting a privacy assessment of the Information Technology Department as part of ISFC's DPDPA 2023 compliance initiative. Could you start by giving us an overview of your department?

Department SPOC: India Shelter’s IT Department serves as a cornerstone of operational efficiency and data security within the organization, providing crucial support to the core business of India Shelter in managing critical applications and systems. As technology continues to evolve, the department's role extends beyond traditional IT functions to driving innovation, enhancing efficiency, and fostering seamless integration of cutting-edge solutions. Information security, data protection, and cybersecurity fall within the ambit of this department.

Department SPOC: This report encapsulates critical insights for As-Is study, derived from the initial interview conducted with the Information Technology Department of India Shelter Finance Corporation Limited (ISFC) for the implementation of ISO/IEC 27001:2022 and compliance with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDPA). The overarching objectives of this interview were dual fold: first, to gain an understanding of the departmental structure, and second, to delve into the core operational processes of the Information Technology Department. Additionally, the interview aimed to assess the existing information security practices, focusing on evaluating adherence to DPDPA requirements and the readiness for ISO/IEC 27001:2022 implementation.
