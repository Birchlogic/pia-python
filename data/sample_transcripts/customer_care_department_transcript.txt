Rajesh Kumar (K&S Digiprotect): Good morning. Thank you for taking the time for this interview. We are conducting a privacy assessment of the Customer Care Department as part of ISFC's DPDPA 2023 compliance initiative. Could you start by giving us an overview of your department?

Department SPOC: The Customer Care team specializes in offering assistance and solutions to customers, adeptly handling their inquiries, concerns, and issues pertaining to ISFC’s products. Their primary objectives include maximizing customer satisfaction, swiftly resolving issues, and facilitating seamless communication between customers and the company. This report encapsulates critical insights for As-Is study, derived from the initial interview conducted with the Customer Care Department of India Shelter Finance Corporation Limited (ISFC) for the implementation of ISO/IEC 27001:2022 and compliance with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDPA).

Department SPOC: The overarching objectives of this interview were dual fold: first, to gain an understanding of the departmental structure, and second, to delve into the core operational processes of the Customer Care Department. Additionally, the interview aimed to assess the existing information security practices, focusing on evaluating adherence to DPDPA requirements and the readiness for ISO/IEC 27001:2022 implementation. By delving into the details of the department's organizational setup, operational workflows, and data handling procedures, this report aims to identify both the strengths and areas of improvement in information security management and DPDPA compliance within the department..

Rajesh Kumar (K&S Digiprotect): Now let's talk about the types of personal data your department collects. What categories of data do you handle, and where is it stored?

Department SPOC: We collect **Customer Contact Details**. Contact information of customers

Rajesh Kumar (K&S Digiprotect): What is the purpose of collecting Customer Contact Details?

Department SPOC: The purpose is: Communication with customers and marketing efforts

Rajesh Kumar (K&S Digiprotect): Where is Customer Contact Details stored?

Department SPOC: Customer Contact Details is stored in Salesforce. Its classification is PII/Sensitive. Retention period is currently not formally defined. Legal basis: Consent
Required.

Department SPOC: We collect **Loan Account Information**. Information about customers' loan accounts, including balance, payment history, and account status

Rajesh Kumar (K&S Digiprotect): What is the purpose of collecting Loan Account Information?

Department SPOC: The purpose is: Assisting customers with loan-related inquiries and managing accounts

Rajesh Kumar (K&S Digiprotect): Where is Loan Account Information stored?

Rajesh Kumar (K&S Digiprotect): Does the Customer Care Department share data with other departments or external parties?

Department SPOC: Yes, we regularly share relevant data with: HR Department, Internal Audit, Compliance Department, Sales Department, IT Department. This is necessary for cross-departmental coordination and regulatory compliance.

Rajesh Kumar (K&S Digiprotect): What are the primary IT systems and tools used by the Customer Care Department?

Department SPOC: We primarily use: Excel, One Drive, Salesforce. These systems are managed by our IT department and access is controlled through role-based access controls.

Rajesh Kumar (K&S Digiprotect): How does the Customer Care Department handle consent management for personal data processing?

Department SPOC: Consent is obtained as part of our standard onboarding and data collection processes. However, I acknowledge that we need to formalize our consent management mechanism in line with DPDPA 2023 requirements. This is something we're actively working on with the Compliance team.
