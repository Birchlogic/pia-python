Rajesh Kumar (K&S Digiprotect): Good morning. Thank you for taking the time for this interview. We are conducting a privacy assessment of the Digital Marketing as part of ISFC's DPDPA 2023 compliance initiative. Could you start by giving us an overview of your department?

Digital Marketing – Siddharth Bhargava: This meticulous approach aims to enhance the conversion rate, maximizing the return on investment on marketing endeavours.
