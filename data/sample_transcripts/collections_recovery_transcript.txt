Rajesh Kumar (K&S Digiprotect): Good morning. Thank you for taking the time for this interview. We are conducting a privacy assessment of the Collections & Recovery as part of ISFC's DPDPA 2023 compliance initiative. Could you start by giving us an overview of your department?

Department SPOC: India Shelter’s Collections & Recovery department is tasked with the responsibility of managing collection operations and facilitating asset recovery processes. As custodians of sensitive customer information, this department plays a pivotal role in ensuring the integrity, confidentiality, and security of data throughout various activities such as case allocation, legal proceedings, and property sales. This report encapsulates critical insights for As-Is study, derived from the initial interview conducted with the Collections & Recovery Department of India Shelter Finance Corporation Limited (ISFC) for the implementation of ISO/IEC 27001:2022 and compliance with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDPA).

Department SPOC: The overarching objectives of this interview were dual fold: first, to gain an understanding of the departmental structure, and second, to delve into the core operational processes of the Collections & Recovery Department. Additionally, the interview aimed to assess the existing information security practices, focusing on evaluating adherence to DPDPA requirements and the readiness for ISO/IEC 27001:2022 implementation. By delving into the details of the department's organizational setup, operational workflows, and data handling procedures, this report aims to identify both the strengths and areas of improvement in information security management and DPDPA compliance within the department..

Rajesh Kumar (K&S Digiprotect): Now let's talk about the types of personal data your department collects. What categories of data do you handle, and where is it stored?

Department SPOC: We collect **Customer Details**. Customer name, address, and pending EMIs

Rajesh Kumar (K&S Digiprotect): What is the purpose of collecting Customer Details?

Department SPOC: The purpose is: Case allocation for collections

Rajesh Kumar (K&S Digiprotect): Where is Customer Details stored?

Department SPOC: Customer Details is stored in iCollect App/
Salesforce/ Excel. Its classification is PII/Sensitive. Retention period is currently not formally defined. Legal basis: Contractual Obligation.

Department SPOC: We collect **Collection Activity Records**. Details of customer visits and calls made by Collection Officers

Rajesh Kumar (K&S Digiprotect): What is the purpose of collecting Collection Activity Records?

Department SPOC: The purpose is: Tracking collection activities

Rajesh Kumar (K&S Digiprotect): Where is Collection Activity Records stored?

Department SPOC: Collection Activity Records is stored in iCollect App/ Salesforce. Its classification is PII/Sensitive. Retention period is currently not formally defined. Legal basis: Contractual Obligation.

Department SPOC: We collect **Finance Portfolio Report**. Loan ID, Customer ID, Customer Name, EMI installment details

Rajesh Kumar (K&S Digiprotect): Does the Collections & Recovery share data with other departments or external parties?

Department SPOC: Yes, we regularly share relevant data with: Legal Department, HR Department, Finance Department, Sales Department, IT Department. This is necessary for cross-departmental coordination and regulatory compliance.

Rajesh Kumar (K&S Digiprotect): What are the primary IT systems and tools used by the Collections & Recovery?

Department SPOC: We primarily use: CRC, Emails, Excel, HRIS, Physical Copies, Salesforce, iCollect App. These systems are managed by our IT department and access is controlled through role-based access controls.

Rajesh Kumar (K&S Digiprotect): How does the Collections & Recovery handle consent management for personal data processing?

Department SPOC: Consent is obtained as part of our standard onboarding and data collection processes. However, I acknowledge that we need to formalize our consent management mechanism in line with DPDPA 2023 requirements. This is something we're actively working on with the Compliance team.

Rajesh Kumar (K&S Digiprotect): Based on our assessment so far, we have some preliminary recommendations. Let me share them with you.

Rajesh Kumar (K&S Digiprotect): Recommendation: Data Masking for Phone Numbers

Rajesh Kumar (K&S Digiprotect): Recommendation: Implement data masking for customer phone numbers in the iCollect app. Ensure that phone numbers are partially obscured in history logs and reports to protect customer privacy.

Rajesh Kumar (K&S Digiprotect): Recommendation: Prevent the download of phone numbers unless strictly necessary and ensure such actions are logged and monitored.

Rajesh Kumar (K&S Digiprotect): Recommendation: Enhanced Security for iCollect Application

Department SPOC: Thank you. These are very helpful. We will take these into consideration and work with our management to implement the necessary changes.
